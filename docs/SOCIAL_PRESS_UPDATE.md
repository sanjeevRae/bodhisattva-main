# Social & Press Enrichment — migration 0015 (2026-11-19)

Content update sourced from the institution's own channels and the
national press, at the owner's direction ("extract information from
their social media and update the contents"). The plan was reviewed by
ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude before implementation; all three approved
with safeguards, which are reflected below.

## What changed
1. **Three new press clippings** (archive 20 → 23), one per outlet
   above. Per the reviewers' unanimous copyright safeguard, the
   outlets' article text is **not** reproduced: each record carries an
   editorial summary written for this archive, the ceremony photograph
   the gallery itself supplied to the press, source, date, byline and
   the external URL of the original article. The 2022 Kathmandu Post
   feature remains the featured item (all three reviewers); the new
   items lead the chronological listing.
2. **Retrospective record** — inauguration account (butter lamp,
   tokens for the 2016 artists, Charya Nritya, the artist's mother and
   wife felicitated, HRH's tour) and a visit paragraph: daily 12–7,
   Rs 200 entry matched by the institution to the Prime Minister's
   Disaster Relief Fund (the institution's own published wording),
   Sunday 4 PM artist tours, gift-shop merchandise, parking. The young
   family performer is mentioned generically — no name, age or photo
   (ChatGPT + Claude).
3. **Babu Kaji record** — the artist-support model as published by the
   gallery itself: works bought and paid for directly from the artist,
   five per cent of sale prices added to the artist, his wife
   felicitated at the inauguration.
4. **2016 record** — one sentence pointing forward to the 2026
   retrospective.
5. **Museum timeline** — first entry is now 1986 (Purna Man Shakya
   begins collecting). **About history** — the 1986 sentence and one
   line on WOW Magazine's "Collectors with Vision" (Dec 2025), text
   only; the supplied event photographs were not used on About
   (Gemini + Claude: event photos lower the curatorial tone; consent
   ambiguity for other guests). **Foundation mission** — the July 2026
   donation of NPR 400,005 to Hiranya Varna Mahavihar (the Golden
   Temple), attributed to the Bodhisattva family as in the source post.
6. **Footer social links** — the round's only code change. Instagram
   and Facebook are `social_instagram` / `social_facebook` public
   settings, rendered as discreet text links in the footer brand
   column, only when set. `social_youtube` is supported by the footer
   but deliberately unset: the official channel URL has not been
   verified (a video link is known; the channel handle is not).
